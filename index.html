Questions people ask

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so each project gets its own fixed quote after a short call. The quote is free.

How long does it take?

It depends on what you need. A website can be up and running in 48 to 72 hours, or take up to two weeks. Apps and custom systems take longer because every feature is tested before launch, usually three to six weeks.

Do I own my site?

Yes. The web address, the content and the site are yours. If you ever move on, you take them with you.

Can you fix the website I already have?

Yes, and the first step is free. Send me your site and I'll tell you three things that are costing you customers. Then you decide what to do next.
